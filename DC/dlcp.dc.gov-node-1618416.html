Charitable Services Business

Any service whose actual or purported purpose is benevolent, philanthropic, patriotic, educational (except religious education), scientific, environmental conservation, civic, or otherwise eleemosynary.
 

Business Activites

  • Charitable Exempt
  • Charitable Solicitation

Requirements

  • Certificate of Occupancy/Home Occupation Permit
  • Charitable Services Business Worksheet
  • Corporate Registration (If Applicable)
  • IRS Determination Letter
  • Certificate of Exemption/FR-164 Exemption
  • Tax Registation & Clean Hands Certificate

Fee Schedule

Charitable Services Business Fees
  • Charitable Exempt: $0
  • Charitable Solicitation:
    • 2-year license - $99.00
    • 4-year license - $198.00
