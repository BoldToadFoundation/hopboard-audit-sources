The Nebraska Secretary of State's office is transitioning to a new business entity search and filing system. We are anticipating the system to be live before the end of the 2026 calendar year. For additional information, please refer to our New Online Business Filing System FAQs.
If you do not have a Subscriber login and file frequently you may benefit from a Nebraska.gov Subscriber account.

This service allows you to electronically submit documents for filing with the Secretary of State. You will be required to upload the document(s) you wish to file and data enter pertinent information. FAQ

View list of available filings

Your document will be reviewed by the Secretary of State’s office and you will receive an email notification regarding your filing once it’s been reviewed. Due to the high volume of submissions at this time it it taking approximately a week for the document to be reviewed. To check the status of your document by clicking on the Filing History option at the top right corner of the page.

Credit Card

Non-subscribers make a selection below and click Continue.

The total payment for online filing will be greater than the statutory filing fee by a minimum of $2.00 per transaction. The total payment covers the operating costs incurred by the Nebraska.gov network manager, Nebraska Interactive, LLC. Fees may be paid using credit card only (Visa, MasterCard, Discover or American Express).

Subscriber

Subscribers please log in above to continue filing

In addition to the statutory fee, total payment includes $2.00 per document filed for operating costs incurred by the Nebraska.gov network manager, Nebraska Interactive, LLC. All fees are charged to the bank account on file for your Nebraska.gov Subscriber account.
