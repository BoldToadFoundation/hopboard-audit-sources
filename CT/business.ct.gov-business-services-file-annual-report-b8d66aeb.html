Frequently asked questions
Get answers to questions you have about the annual report, including when it’s due and whether you’ll receive a reminder to file it.

About annual reports

An annual report has basic information about your company. You file it every year so we have up-to-date facts. It doesn’t include any financial information.

These types of companies must file an annual report:

  • Stock and non-stock corporation
  • Limited liability company (LLC)
  • Limited liability partnership (LLP)
  • Limited partnership (LP)

Depending on the type of business, the information you report may include these:

  • Business email and street address
  • Owner’s mailing and street addresses
  • Registered agent’s name, email, mobile phone number, and business, residence, and mailing addresses
  • Principal’s name, title, email, and business and residence addresses
  • North American Industry Classification System (NAICS)
  • Foreign entity information
Visit Domestic Annual Report Forms Fees to see the fee for each type of business.
If your report is overdue, you can’t get a certificate of legal existence, which may make it difficult to do business. If you don’t file your annual report, the Secretary of the State’s Office may dissolve your business.
