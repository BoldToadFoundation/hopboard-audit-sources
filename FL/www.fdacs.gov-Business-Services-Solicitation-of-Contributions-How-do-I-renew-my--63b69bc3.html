How do I renew my registration?

The easiest option is to renew online. If you have never registered online, you will need to select “Create Account” and follow the steps to create a username and password. 

If you prefer to submit an application by mail, please use the preprinted renewal application that will be mailed to you approximately 30 days in advance of your expiration date. If you did not receive the renewal application, you may call FDACS at 1-800-HELP-FLA (435-7352) or email CSWebmaster@FDACS.gov to request that one be emailed or mailed to you. 

The registration forms on our website are for “new” organizations only.

Disaster-Related Charities

Charitable organizations that raise $50,000 or more in contributions during the aftermath of natural disasters or other crises must submit financial information regarding contributions and program service expenses on a quarterly basis, except for charitable organizations that have been registered with FDACS for at least four consecutive years.

FDACS will post a notice on its website, FDACS.gov, of each disaster or crisis subject to the reporting requirements of Section 496.4072, Florida Statutes, within 10 days after the disaster or crisis.
