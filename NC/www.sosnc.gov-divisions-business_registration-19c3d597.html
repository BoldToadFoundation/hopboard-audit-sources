North Carolina Scam Alert
Vendors are aggressively pushing “priority,” “early” Annual Report filing, charging fees on top of the filing fees required by statute. Save time and money – file online after the first of the year by clicking on the yellow button titled “File your Business Annual Report.”
Military Deployment Filing Extension (SL 2026-59)
If more than 50% of the business is owned by a deployed service member, you can file an Affidavit of Deployment prior to deployment. The annual report will be due 90 days after the deployment ends.

What We Do

The duty of the Secretary of State is to ensure uniform compliance with the statutes governing the creation of business entities, record the information required to be kept as a public record, and provide that information to the public. The Business Registration Division acts in an administrative capacity only and cannot give legal advice.
