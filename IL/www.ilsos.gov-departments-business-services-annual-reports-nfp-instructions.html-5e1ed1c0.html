Information for Filing an NFP Annual Report Online

Guidelines

  • A Domestic Corporation (incorporated in Illinois) may file an annual report electronically.
  • A Foreign Corporation (incorporated outside Illinois) may not file an annual report electronically.
  • A maximum of six officers and seven directors can be entered electronically.
  • An electronically transmitted annual report is considered an original filing.
  • All penalties incurred as of the current date must be paid along with the filing fee at the time of filing.
  • If you need to change the registered agent and/or registered office, you must:
    • File the annual report indicating no changes.
    • Change the registered agent or registered office online or complete Form NFP 105.10/105.20.
