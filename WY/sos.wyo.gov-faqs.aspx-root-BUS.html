Annual Reports
Q. Is an annual report or annual renewal required in Wyoming? If so, when is it due?
To remain in good standing each business entity, domestic and foreign, is required to file an annual report and pay a fee based on its assets located and employed in Wyoming or is assessed a flat filing fee. The annual report due date for all registered business entities (with the exception of Statutory Trusts) is based on the anniversary month of formation. For example, if your business registers or qualifies on January 15th, your annual report due date will be January 1st of each year.

Statutory Trusts are required to file an annual report due on or before January 2nd of each year regardless of its formation or qualification date.

An annual report may be filed up to one hundred and twenty (120) days before its due date.
Q. How are annual report fees calculated for domestic and foreign profit corporations, limited liability companies, limited partnerships, and registered limited liability partnerships?
These entities use the same annual license tax schedule and pay a license tax based on all assets located and employed in Wyoming. The tax is $60 or two-tenths of one mill on the dollar ($.0002), whichever is greater.
Q. What will my annual license tax fee be if I have $200,000 in assets located and employed in Wyoming?
The tax is $60. An entity with $300,000 or less in assests pays $60. If filed online, a convenience fee is assessed by the payment processor based on the total license tax due.
Q. What will my annual license tax be if I have $1,210,000 in assets located and employed in Wyoming?
The tax is $242 ($1,210,000 x .0002 = $242). If filed online, a convenience fee is assessed by the payment processor based on the total license tax due.
Q. How are annual reports fees calculated for domestic and foreign nonprofits, statutory trusts and statutory foundations?
Nonprofits are required to pay an annual report fee of $25; Statutory Trusts and Statutory Foundations are required to pay an annual report fee of $100. These entities are not required to provide information on their assets located and employed in Wyoming. If filed online, a convenience fee is assessed by the payment processor based on the total license tax due.
Q. Can I file my annual report online?
Yes, annual reports may be filed online using Visa or Mastercard credit cards or Visa or Mastercard authorized debit cards. Registered business entities which are active can use the Annual Report Wizard to file an original annual report. If your annual report fee is greater than $500, e-filing is not permitted.
Q. Can I amend my annual report?
To change officers and/or directors of a profit corporation or nonprofit corporation, you may submit an Update of Officers/Directors form. This form may only be used if an annual report is currently on file. For all other changes, please contact the Business Division at Business@wyo.gov.
Q. Do I get reminders when my annual report is coming due?
Courtesy email reminders are sent sixty (60), thirty (30) and ten (10) days prior to a business entity's annual report due date using the email address provided to us. The annual report reminder comes from SOS_AnnualReports@wyo.gov.
Q. What happens if I miss my annual report due date?
The entity will be deemed delinquent on the second day of the month following its due date. If the annual report is not filed within sixty (60) days following the due date, the entity will be administratively dissolved.
Doing Business in Wyoming
Q. If I am doing business in Wyoming and I am incorporated in another state or jurisdiction, do I need to qualify to do business in Wyoming?
Yes, Wyoming law requires entities doing business in this state to qualify by completing and filing a Certificate of Authority Application form with the Business Division. Click here to access the forms.
Q. If I am an out-of-state entity doing business in Wyoming without authority from the Secretary of State, will I be penalized?
Yes, any business entity doing business in Wyoming without authority will be liable for a penalty in the amount of $5,000, fees and license taxes, plus interest of eighteen percent (18%) for the years or parts thereof during which it transacted business in this state without a certificate of authority, along with reasonable audit expenses and reasonable attorney fees.
Q. What if I am incorporated in another state and want to do business in Wyoming?
File an Application for Certificate of Authority form with this office. Click here to access forms.
Q. Do I need to register my out-of-state-entity in Wyoming?
Refer to W.S. 17-16-1501 and additional information available on this site. Then, if you are still in doubt, you should consult legal counsel to determine if you are transacting business within Wyoming in accordance with Wyoming statutes. The Business Division is unable to provide legal advice of any kind.
Q. Do I need to register my out-of-state nonprofit corporation in Wyoming?
Wyoming has no state requirements for charities and fund raisers, however your corporation may be required to qualify as a foreign corporation in Wyoming. Refer to W.S. 17-19-1501 and additional information available on this site. Then, if you are still in doubt, you should consult legal counsel to determine if you are transacting business within Wyoming in accordance with Wyoming statutes. The Business Division is unable to provide legal advice of any kind.

Q. How do I reinstate my company?
An entity dissolved/revoked for failure to file annual reports within the past two (2) years, and which has no other delinquencies, may file a reinstatement online, or generate reinstatement forms for mail-in processing. Entities having multiple delinquencies will be provided the appropriate filing form for mail-in processing. Click here to begin the process.

Wyoming statutes do not allow for reinstatement after two (2) years in an administratively dissolved standing.
Q. Do I need a sales/use tax license?
Please contact the Department of Revenue.
Q. Is a general contractor license required in Wyoming?
For information on electrical contractors, please contact the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. For information on general contractors, please contact the local jurisdiction where the construction activity is to take place.
Q. If I am a general or prime contractor, what are the financial and bond requirements?
For information, please contact Department of Revenue.
Q. Do I need specfic license or business permit in Wyoming?
Other than registration with the Secretary of State, a company may be in need of a specific business license or business permit based on their business purpose. For more information on specific licensing and permitting, please contact the City Clerk's Office in the Wyoming city you are conducting business.
Q. As an impact employer, am I required to make a cash prepayment to the unemployment insurance fund? What do I need to do if I employ myself or others?
For information, please contact Department of Workforce Services, Unemployment Tax Division.
Q. If I am a nonresident employer, am I required to post a bond with Workers' Compensation? What do I need to do if I employ myself or others?
For information, please contact Department of Workforce Services, Workers' Compensation Division.
Q. How long does it take to form a business entity in Wyoming?
Some domestic business entity types can file online. When filed online, the business entity is active immediately. Out-of-state entities filing a Certificate of Authority, Articles of Continuance or Articles of Domestication are required to submit their application and appropriate filing fee through the mail. Documents received through the mail are reviewed in the order they are received with a maximum processing time of fifteen (15) business days.
Q. Do I need an attorney to form a business entity?
No, but you should consult with a legal advisor concerning legal questions or issues. The Business Division is unable to provide legal advice of any kind.
Q. How do I register a “Doing Business As” (DBA) name?
In Wyoming it is not mandatory to register a DBA name. However, you may register a DBA by filing an Application for Registration of Trade Name.

Please Refer to How To Trace Old Stock for more information.

Information may be obtained by contacting the Wyoming Small Business Development Center or the Wyoming Business Council.
Q. Does my business need to comply with Wyoming's Securities Laws?
All securities, including limited partnership interests, must be cleared for sale or be exempt from registration under Wyoming’s Uniform Securities Act W.S. 174-201 or 301 before being sold to residents in this state. Information may be obtained by contacting the Compliance Division of this office.
Q. Where can I obtain a copy of Wyoming’s business entity laws?
The statutes can be obtained from the Legislative Service Office.
Online Searches
Q. Does Wyoming have business entity information available online?
Bulk business entity information including the name and address of the registered agent, officers and directors, status, standing, and mailing address, can be purchased through a subscription. Click here to find the Business Database Download form and agreement.
Q. How can I obtain copies of business documents?
The quickest way to obtain plain copies is to visit our Business Center and conduct a search for the business entity. Once on the business record, click on the “History” option. If a PDF icon appears to the right of the filing or amendment, an image of the filing/amendment is available through that icon. If you still aren’t able to obtain all of the documents you need, please email your request to SOSRequest@wyo.gov.
