Charities

 

Annual filing form

The Charities Bureau has an online portal for submitting charities’ annual financial filings. This interactive version of the CHAR500 offers a streamlined process that allows uploading of all required documents, ePayment, and eSignature. Filings submitted via the online portal will be processed and posted promptly to the bureau’s online registry.

If you have any questions, please email Charities.Bureau@ag.ny.gov.
