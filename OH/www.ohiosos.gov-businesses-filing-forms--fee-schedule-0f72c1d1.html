Foreign Cooperative Filing Forms

Registration Requirements for Foreign Cooperatives in Ohio

According to Ohio Revised Code 1729.76, a foreign cooperative association may conduct appropriate activities within the State of Ohio once it has met the general requirements that apply to all foreign corporations seeking to do business in the state.

Foreign cooperatives registering in Ohio must determine independently whether to file as a for-profit or nonprofit foreign corporation.

Shares Fee Information

Corporation Filing Fees in Ohio

The Ohio Secretary of State calculates corporation filing fees based on the number of shares authorized when forming a business.

Minimum Filing Fee

  • The minimum filing fee for corporations authorizing one (1) to 990 shares is $99.

Additional Fees for Corporations Authorizing More Than 990 Shares

For corporations authorizing more than 990 shares, additional fees apply on a graduated scale. 
This means the total filing fee increases as the number of authorized shares increases.

Nonprofit Corporations

Nonprofit entities do not calculate filing fees based on shares. 
Instead, their filing fees are fixed, regardless of the number of members or capital structure.

Filing Fee Calculator

To determine the total filing fee for your corporation, use the Shares Calculator provided by the Ohio Secretary of State. 
The calculator helps ensure you pay the correct fee based on the number of authorized shares.
