Charities, Nonprofits & Fundraising

Professional Fundraisers

Professional fundraisers are outside entities who raise money on behalf of charities. These fundraisers must register with the Office of the Attorney General pursuant to Iowa Code Chapter 13C.

Who Must Register? Registration Fees Charitable Disclosures Form Resources Contact
Definition

What is a professional fundraiser?

A professional fundraiser is a person or outside entity who, for compensation, solicits contributions in Iowa for a charitable organization other than themselves.

Professional fundraiser under Iowa law

“Professional fundraiser” means any person who, for compensation, solicits contributions in Iowa for a charitable organization other than the person.

Included

Outside entities or persons paid to solicit contributions in Iowa for a charitable organization.

Not included

A person whose sole responsibility is mailing fundraising literature; a lawyer, investment counselor, or banker who advises charitable giving; or a bona fide salaried officer, employee, or volunteer of a charitable organization.

Important Note

Registration applies to professional fundraisers

Note: The registration requirement is only for professional fundraisers. Charitable organizations that do not utilize the services of a professional fundraiser to raise funds are not required to register under this chapter of the Iowa Code. Professional fundraisers must disclose each charity for which they raise funds on the Professional Fundraiser Registration Form, regardless of where the funds are solicited.

Registration Fees

Professional fundraiser registration fees

Professional fundraisers must pay an annual registration fee when registering with the Office of the Attorney General.

Annual registration fee

$10.00 per year

Online processing fee

$1.00 processing fee if registration is processed online

Note: Online filing options are listed in the Forms & Filing Options section below.

Required Reporting

Charitable organization disclosures & significant changes reporting

Professional fundraisers may be required to file additional disclosures and update information previously submitted to the Office of the Attorney General when fundraising activities or registration information materially changes.

📢

Charitable Organization Disclosure Form

This disclosure form is generally used to identify and disclose charitable organizations for which fundraising activities are being conducted.

Professional fundraisers must disclose each charity for which they solicit contributions, regardless of where the funds are solicited.

🔄

Significant Changes Reporting

Updated reporting may be required when significant information changes after registration or disclosure materials have already been submitted.

Examples may include changes involving fundraising campaigns, charity relationships, contact information, or other material registration information.

⚠️

When reporting may be required

Reporting obligations may apply before, during, or after fundraising activity depending on the nature of the solicitation activity and any changes to previously filed information.

Professional fundraisers should ensure filings remain accurate and current throughout fundraising operations conducted in Iowa.

Important: Filing obligations may vary depending on the structure of the fundraising relationship and the information previously submitted to the Office of the Attorney General. Professional fundraisers should review all registration and disclosure requirements carefully before conducting solicitation activity in Iowa.
