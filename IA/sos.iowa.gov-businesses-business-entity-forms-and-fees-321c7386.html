Business Entity Forms and Fees

  1. Home

There are many filings in our office that do not have a form associated with them. If the name of the form is linked, there is a form provided for you to fill out. If only the code section is linked, that will be a document you will need to draft; the document you draft must meet the requirements laid out in the given section of the Iowa Code. If you need assistance drafting a document in compliance with the Code because there isn’t a form for it, definitely speak with your own legal counsel.

Some filings may be applicable to both domestic and foreign entities. If you have any questions, please contact our office at 515-281-5204.

Many of these forms can be submitted online through our Fast Track Filing System!


Just go to filings.sos.iowa.gov to get started with the online filing process!

Biennial Report

Click here to expand or collapse this section

Businesses, both domestic and foreign, will file a biennial report with the Office of the Iowa Secretary of State every other year.

The biennial schedule is as follows:

Profit Corporations: Every Even numbered year beginning January 1st and due by April 1st. The filing fee is $60.

LLCs, LLPs, and Nonprofit Corporations: Every Odd numbered year beginning January 1st and due by April 1st. The fee is $30* by online filing or $45* by paper.

*Nonprofit corporations do not have a filing fee for their biennial reports.

To file your report, you can file directly online through our Fast Track Filing System. You can also print it off from Fast Track and mail it in to our office if you would prefer to file by paper.

There is a walkthrough on how to file online available by clicking this link.

If you would like to file by paper, in Step 2 of the walkthrough where it shows the entity search, be sure the "yes" is highlighted and it will bring up a PDF document for you to print.
