Apply for Exemption from Registration

All charitable organizations must register in the State of Hawaii with the Attorney General's office if they solicit funds unless covered by an exemption in the law. To apply for exemption from registration, complete & submit the following form.

Type of Exemption

The applicant must submit substantiation that the applicant is a "church", an integrated auxiliary of a church, or nondenominational ministries, interdenominational and ecumenical organizations, and other entities whose principal purpose is the study or advancement of religion. If the applicant is neither a church nor integrated auxiliary, please submit a copy of your IRS determination letter with this application. Please consult IRS Publication No. 1828 for guidance on religious corporations and organizations exempt from taxation.
