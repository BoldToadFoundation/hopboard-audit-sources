  • Forms That Must Be Completed and Submitted To The Hawaii Attorney General's Office From This Site:
    • Charities that solicit contributions in Hawaii must complete a one-time REGISTRATION with Hawaii Attorney General's Office or obtain an exemption from registration. Registration is Free. Click HERE to learn more about Hawaii's registration requirements.
    • Registered charities must use this site to complete and transmit a required annual HAWAII TRANSMITTAL (also known as the Annual Transmittal Form). Click HERE to learn more about the State of Hawaii Registered Charity Annual Reporting Requirements.

  • What are my options in filing my organization's IRS Form 990/990-EZ for the Hawaii Transmittal?
    • The Form 990/990-EZ may be completed on our website and electronically transmitted to the IRS at the same time that the annual Hawaii Transmittal data is electronically transmitted to Hawaii.
    • The Form 990/990-EZ may be completed on our website or through any other system, uploaded as a PDF file, and then transmitted with the annual Hawaii Transmittal.
    • The Form 990/990-EZ, if initially done by hand, can be converted to PDF format, uploaded, and then transmitted with the annual Hawaii Transmittal.
