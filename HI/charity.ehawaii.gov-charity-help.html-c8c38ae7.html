Charities Amnesty Program: Extended to October 31, 2026

Eligible charitable organizations that are behind on required filings or fees may use this amnesty period to return to compliance. Organizations that complete all required filings and satisfy program requirements by October 31, 2026, may qualify for waiver of certain late fees and penalties.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register my charitable organization on this website?

No, to register your charitable organization, go to the Hawaii Charity Registration System. To read more information about registering, go to the Tax and Charities Division website.

Can I file an annual financial report for my charitable organization on this website?

No, to file your annual financial report, go to the Hawaii Charity Registration System.

To learn more information about filing an annual financial report go to the Tax and Charities Division website.

How do I pay my annual fee?
  1. Go to the pay fees page
  2. Enter your FEIN
  3. Select ‘Continue’
  4. Select ‘Pay Annual Fee’ for the appropriate tax year
  5. Select your payment method
  6. Enter you name and title
  7. Select ‘Continue to Payment’
  8. Enter all the required payment information
  9. Select ‘Continue’
  10. Review your information and select ‘Pay Now’
  11. Select ‘Continue’
  12. Print a copy of your receipt
  13. Select ‘Done’
Why am I being charged a late fee?

Under Hawaii law, the Attorney General may assess late fees of $20 per day up to a maximum fee of $1,000 against a charitable organization for failure to timely pay its annual fee.

Where do commercial co-ventures list their charitable sales promotions?

Charitable sales promotions can be found using the search.

  1. Go to the search
  2. Select the ‘Charitable Sales Promotions’ tab
  3. Enter your search criteria
  4. At the bottom of the page select ‘Search’
  5. Select a charitable sales promotion from the list of results to view more details
