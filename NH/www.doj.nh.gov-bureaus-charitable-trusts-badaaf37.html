Charitable Trusts Unit

More than 15,000 charitable organizations are active in New Hampshire, providing a multitude of critical services to the public. The Charitable Trusts Unit's mission is to protect the public's interest in those organizations and the funds that have been committed to them. CTU accomplishes that mission through its registration and reporting requirements; publication of educational materials; and robust investigation and enforcement.

Know before you donate. CTU maintains a directory of more than 12,000 charities that are properly registered to solicit charitable funds in New Hampshire, as well as a listing of fundraising counsel and paid solicitors.

CTU's Roles

Complaints & Investigations
Pursuant to RSA 7:19, et seq., CTU possesses both criminal and civil enforcement authority to hold accountable those who misuse charitable assets in New Hampshire. Through its statutory and common-law authority, CTU reviews and investigates numerous complaints every year related to charitable organizations.
Municipalities
Most New Hampshire municipalities hold donated charitable funds in trust. CTU oversees those funds and ensures that municipal trustees of trust funds engage in appropriate accounting and adherence to charitable restrictions.
Conservation Easements
Many New Hampshire landowners generously choose to donate easements over their land for conservation purposes. CTU ensures that the charitable organizations that hold donated conservation easements in trust for the public enforce the restrictions thereon.
Cy Pres & Modifications
CTU ensures that those who hold charitable funds use that money consistent with the donors' stated intents. Over time, however, some charitable trust fund restrictions become impossible, impractical to achieve, or simply obsolete. CTU works with charitable fiduciaries to modify charitable trust fund restrictions through a process known as cy pres. And to ensure that charitable funds are used for charity--not administrative overhead--CTU works with charitable fiduciaries to terminate, modify, or move funds that are no longer financially practical to maintain.
Healthcare Reviews & Reports
CTU oversees many charitable organizations that provide healthcare services. Under RSA 7:19-b, CTU investigates and reviews all acquisition transactions involving charitable organizations that provide healthcare services. Some organizations must also provide reporting about community benefits and health needs.
